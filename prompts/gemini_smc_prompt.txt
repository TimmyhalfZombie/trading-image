CORE METHODOLOGY (sourced from professional ICT/SMC traders)
═══════════════════════════════════════════

This analysis follows the ICT (Inner Circle Trader) multi-timeframe entry model:
1. Establish higher-timeframe bias using market structure (HH/HL = bullish, LH/LL = bearish)
2. Confirm direction on the mid-timeframe and identify Points of Interest (POI)
3. Execute on the lower timeframe after liquidity sweep + market structure shift + displacement
4. Use the ICT Power of 3 (AMD) framework: Accumulation → Manipulation → Distribution

KEY SMC DEFINITIONS:
- Break of Structure (BOS): Price closes beyond a swing high (bullish) or swing low (bearish) with FULL CANDLE BODY, confirming trend continuation.
- Change of Character (CHoCH): Price closes beyond a swing point in the OPPOSITE direction of the current trend, signaling potential reversal.
- Market Structure Shift (MSS): A CHoCH accompanied by strong displacement (aggressive candle movement), confirming institutional intent.
- Order Block (OB): The last opposing candle before a BOS or MSS that moved with displacement. Bullish OB = last bearish candle before strong up-move. Bearish OB = last bullish candle before strong down-move.
- Fair Value Gap (FVG): A 3-candle pattern where candle 1's wick and candle 3's wick do NOT overlap, creating a price inefficiency. Price tends to return to fill this gap.
- Liquidity Sweep: Price wicks beyond a swing high/low (grabbing stop-losses) but FAILS to close beyond it, then reverses. This is institutional "stop hunting."
- Displacement: Strong, impulsive candle movement away from a level, often leaving FVGs behind. Confirms institutional commitment.
- Draw on Liquidity (DOL): The target where price is heading — typically equal highs/lows, session extremes (London/NY highs/lows), or previous day high/low (PDH/PDL).
- Premium/Discount Zone: Divide the current swing range by 50%. BUY setups should be in DISCOUNT (below 50%). SELL setups should be in PREMIUM (above 50%).
- Optimal Trade Entry (OTE): The 0.62–0.79 Fibonacci retracement zone within a swing, where institutional entries are most likely.
